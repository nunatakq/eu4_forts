# Third-party components

- **highs.js / highs.wasm** — HiGHS linear/MIP solver compiled to WebAssembly by the
  highs-js project (https://github.com/lovasoa/highs-js). highs-js and HiGHS
  (https://highs.dev) are distributed under the MIT License.
- **Original application** — EU4FLO by Krollbotid (https://github.com/Krollbotid/EU4FLO),
  MIT License (see LICENSE). This web version is a port of its parsers, adjacency
  builder, renderer and optimization model.

Europa Universalis IV and its content are property of Paradox Interactive. This app
ships no game assets; it only reads files from the user's own installation, locally in
the browser.
